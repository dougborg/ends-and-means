# Approach change

An Approach is a recognizable configuration of Concepts, attributed Ends, and
proposed Means. It is not the ontology root, an exclusive ideological family,
or a timeless label for a country or government.

Include a clear `scope`. Connect core, adjacent, peripheral, or contested
Concepts through attributed relationships. Represent overlapping umbrellas as
qualified Collection memberships, and attach Ends, Means, Challenges, and Cases
only through explicit sourced relationships. Do not inherit positions from a
Collection or imply that missing links are positions.
