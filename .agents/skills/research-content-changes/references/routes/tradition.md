# Tradition change

A tradition is a historically situated family of ideas and institutional
arguments, not a country, party, slogan, or internally uniform blueprint.

Include `description`, `scope`, `variants`, `distinctions`, and `commonQuestions`.
Identify representative thinkers or texts through sourced claims and explain
contested membership. Link only researched Ends, Means, Challenges, and cases;
do not fill a comparison grid or imply that missing links are positions.
