# Bounded case change

A case is a specific historical or contemporary episode used to examine a
mechanism under named conditions. A whole country, civilization, or century is
usually too broad.

Include `name`, `startDate`, `endDate`, `location`, `scope`, `selectionRationale`,
`conditions`, `outcomes`, `claimIds`, `rivalExplanations`, and
`transferLimitations`. Link traditions cautiously and institutional Means
directly evidenced in the episode. Explain mixed arrangements and changes.
