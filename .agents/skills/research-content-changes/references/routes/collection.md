# Collection change

A Collection is an editorial discovery grouping, not a superclass. State its
inclusion rule and editorial purpose. Membership may be widely accepted,
qualified, or contested and may overlap other Collections.

Collections may group Concepts, Approaches, other Collections, or institutional
Means families. Membership never confers an End, Means, domain, Placement,
assessment, or another membership. Author every substantive relationship
explicitly. For a Means family such as central planning, use a Collection of
concrete Means records and describe authority, scope, information, targets,
revision, enforcement, and ownership context in focused Statements attached to
each Means through typed `specified-by` relationships.
