# Depiction change

A Depiction is an attributed interpretation of a fictional political economy
in a specific fictional Work. Its `workId` must resolve to a Work whose
`workType` is `fiction`, and its scope must identify what part or presentation
of the Work is being interpreted.

Use typed `depicts` relationships and focused editorial-interpretation
Statements to relate a Depiction to Concepts, Approaches, Ends, or Means. A
fiction-derived Source may ground what the Work depicts, provide context, or
qualify an empirical claim, but it cannot support or challenge an empirical
observation, Case outcome, or assessment. Eligible evidence must resolve
through the Source to an actual non-fiction Work and independently support every
promoted empirical Statement.
