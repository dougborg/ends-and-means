# Means change

A Means is an institutional mechanism or arrangement: who may act, through what
rules, with which resources, oversight, and enforcement.

Include a clear `description` and `institutionalForm`. Put claims about actors,
decision rules, enforcement, conditions, and failure modes in focused sourced
Statements rather than treating them as universal fields of the Means. Use
typed relationships to distinguish an Approach advocating a Means from a Case
using it. Distinguish formal design from rules-in-use and observed operation.
