# Statement or interpretation change

A statement is one contestable proposition in an argument trace. It may be an
empirical claim, attributed value, causal hypothesis, or editorial
interpretation; do not combine several propositions into one record.

Include `statementKind`, `text`, `claimIds`, and `interpretationStatus`. For a
causal or interpretive statement, also include `rationale`,
`rivalInterpretations`, and `conditions`. Add only relationships needed to
understand the statement; there is no required rectangular coverage.
