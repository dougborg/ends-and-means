# Statement or interpretation change

A statement is one contestable proposition in an argument trace. It may be an
empirical claim, attributed value, attributed institutional proposal, causal
hypothesis, or editorial interpretation; do not combine several propositions
into one record. Use `attributed-proposal` when a named actor or source proposes
an institutional design. Do not label the proposal as an `attributed-value`,
which is reserved for an actor's End or normative commitment.

Include the canonical `statementKind` and one focused `text` claim. Connect the
Statement to Sources through `cites` relationships with an evidentiary `role`
and precise `locator`; do not embed citations or duplicate claim IDs on the
Statement. Use separate Statements for rival causal or interpretive claims. Add
only relationships needed to understand the claim; there is no required
rectangular coverage.
