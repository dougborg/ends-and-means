Executive dynastic monarchy combines governing authority in the monarch with participation by other ruling-family members in important offices and decisions.
Herb's category is an attributed analysis of selected dynastic monarchies, not a synonym for every monarchy, absolute power, or personal rule.

## design

Saudi Arabia's Basic Law states that the King shall be prime minister, but a September 2022 royal order appointed Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman to that office as an express exception.
Herb's accessible chapter supplies the narrower analytical distinction: in dynastic monarchy, ruling-family members participate broadly in government, including through cabinet and other senior offices.
Formal executive authority, succession, family membership, and rules in use remain distinct questions.
