United States controlled-materials allocation reconciled selected production programs with supplies of steel, copper, and aluminum from April 1943 through September 1945.

## how-did-materials-allocation-fit

The Controlled Materials Plan supplied the rules for this bounded approach.
Its allotment chain coordinated selected critical metals and does not describe labor, prices, procurement as a whole, or goods outside CMP’s scope.

## what-does-the-approach-not-prove

The approach does not make the whole wartime economy one centrally planned system.
Nor does official reporting of smoother CMP operation resolve later disputes about causation or whose priorities prevailed.
