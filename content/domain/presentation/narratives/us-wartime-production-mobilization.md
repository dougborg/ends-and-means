United States wartime production mobilization combined military procurement, civilian production controls, private contracting, and scarce-material allocation from 1942 through 1945.

## how-did-materials-allocation-fit

The Controlled Materials Plan was one strong component of that wider approach rather than its definition.
Its allotment chain coordinated selected critical metals while other institutions handled labor, prices, procurement, and goods outside CMP’s scope.

## what-does-the-approach-not-prove

The bounded approach does not make the whole wartime economy one centrally planned system.
Nor does official reporting of smoother CMP operation resolve later disputes about causation or whose priorities prevailed.
