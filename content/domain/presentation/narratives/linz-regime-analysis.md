Linz's regime analysis is a scholarly comparison of ideal types, not a political movement, a regime's self-description, or an automatic label for a country.

## authoritarian-type

The authoritarian ideal type combines limited pluralism, restricted mobilization, weak or absent guiding ideology, and leadership within unclear but generally predictable limits.

## totalitarian-type

For the contrasting type, Linz asks whether rule converges around one political center, demands fidelity to an encompassing doctrine, and organizes participation from above.
Historical cases can fit either type incompletely, so the categories do not transfer all properties to every classified regime.
