Nationalism can describe attachments to a nation, programs for political authority, or movements pursuing self-determination.
It does not make nation, state, country, people, ethnicity, citizenship, and homeland synonyms, and a nationalist label does not establish institutions or inclusion in practice.

## meanings

The Stanford account separates national identity from action for self-determination and distinguishes nations from states.
Anderson instead emphasizes how people imagine a limited and sovereign political community, while Chatterjee asks how anticolonial nationalism remained constrained by categories it opposed.
Tagore's critique targets an organized political and commercial Nation rather than treating every form of collective belonging as nationalism.

## statuses

Self-determination may be pursued through an independent state, autonomy, federation, or other arrangements.
Formal sovereignty and effective control are also separate: having a state does not prove that a community determines its important choices.

## boundaries

National fraternity can be asserted amid unequal power and exclusion.
Race, caste, class, gender, religion, language, region, citizenship, and Indigenous status require their own evidence; unity or self-description cannot answer who participated or benefited.

## bounded-practice

In 1897, Hui Aloha ʻĀina collected 21,269 Native Hawaiian signatures on a bilingual petition opposing annexation.
The campaign helped defeat a treaty, but Congress annexed Hawaiʻi by joint resolution in 1898.
At Bandung in 1955, twenty-nine governments joined anticolonial principles to sovereignty and non-intervention; Ghana's 1957 legal independence supplies a distinct status change.
These episodes show different national, Indigenous, anticolonial, and international claims, not one sequence or definition.

## comparisons

Compare who claims national membership, which status they seek, which organization speaks, and which rules operate.
Patriotism, citizenship, anticolonial politics, and Indigenous nationhood may overlap nationalism, but none can be inferred from another.
