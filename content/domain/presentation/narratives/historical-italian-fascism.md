Historical Italian Fascism was a movement, party, doctrine, and governing project whose organizational stages and claims must not be collapsed into one timeless label.

## doctrine-and-organization

Mussolini and Gentile presented an anti-liberal and anti-Marxist doctrine centered on state and nation.
That self-description records a claim, while the movement founded in 1919, the later party, and the coalition government formed in 1922 remained distinct organizations and moments.

## bounded-rule

The 1925–1943 case begins with consolidated dictatorship, not with the movement's founding or Mussolini's initial coalition government.
It therefore supports a bounded comparison without making every Italian institution an embodiment of doctrine.
