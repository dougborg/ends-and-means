# Ends and Means roadmap

The [project vision](docs/project-vision.md) defines the durable product
direction.
GitHub issues and milestones are the authoritative backlog; the GitHub
Project holds only active delivery work and a small ready queue.

## Product sequence

Development follows the learner journey rather than expanding every canonical
entity kind evenly.
The three work tracks advance together with no more than one implementation
item active in each track.

### Corpus

Build complete, evidence-backed learning journeys from larger anchors down to
institutional mechanisms and bounded practice.

1. Establish Socialism and Communism as high-level subject anchors with clear
   boundaries and links to existing reviewed material.
2. Establish Democracy and Republic, then Anarchism, using the same learner
   completeness standard.
3. In the first research tranches, add bounded, society-specific slices across
   Indigenous and kinship-based governance, stateless organization, nomadic or
   pastoral societies, maritime or island polities, city-states, empires,
   colonial formations, and hybrid institutions.
   Use multiple appropriate evidence traditions and reject universal
   “tribal” or “primitive-stage” classifications.
4. Expand institutional subjects such as market socialism, social ownership,
   and central planning without turning them into undifferentiated ideologies.
5. Add bounded historical and ongoing Cases across several places and periods.
6. Add Depictions after the empirical and interpretive boundary has been tested
   on a useful corpus.

Corpus progress is measured by complete learning paths, evidence breadth,
cross-layer relationships, and honest open questions—not raw page count alone.

### Reader experience

1. Design learner-first global navigation around Explore, Cases, Compare, and
   Questions, with Sources and Method as a quieter trust layer.
2. Define and implement validated SubjectGuide composition without changing
   canonical entity identity.
3. Build Explore discovery, entity labels, guide entry phrases, and search
   around the first anchor corpus.
4. Make long pages easy to scan with consistent shells, semantic measures,
   useful headings, compact rhythm, and generated on-page navigation.
5. Reorient Compare around recognizable reader questions and supported shared
   dimensions.
6. Add reusable visual identity and theme support after the navigation and page
   composition are stable.

### Platform and process

1. Enforce content integrity, canonical-only publication, narrative quality,
   and evidence traceability in the authoring harness.
2. Detect delivery drift: excess work in progress, stale states, an oversized or
   empty ready queue, and divergence between local and hosted checks.
3. Keep dependencies, security analysis, coverage, complexity, accessibility,
   and deployment verification current and ratcheted.
4. Audit repository skills when workflows change, keeping instructions concise
   and consistent with contributor documentation.

## Milestone outcomes

### [Plural graph foundation](https://github.com/dougborg/ends-and-means/milestone/1)

The canonical graph preserves identity, model boundaries, typed relationships,
bounded evidence, and one publication source.
Statements, Sources, Research Obligations, Comparison Dimensions, and
Placements are independently reviewable and deterministically compiled.
Boundary fixtures prove that varied social formations can be represented
without universal-stage categories or nation-state assumptions.

### [Explore and Compare core](https://github.com/dougborg/ends-and-means/milestone/2)

Readers can complete the first high-level subject journeys, move between ideas
and bounded practice, inspect evidence without losing the narrative, and make
supported comparisons through an accessible, consistent interface.
The first corpus includes society-specific perspectives from more than one
geographic region, organizational form, and evidence tradition.

### [Advanced comparison and change over time](https://github.com/dougborg/ends-and-means/milestone/3)

Readers can filter, compare multiple dimensions, and examine institutional
change over time without hidden scope changes, forced symmetry, or aggregate
political scores.

### [Contribution and editorial governance](https://github.com/dougborg/ends-and-means/milestone/4)

Corrections, new evidence, appeals, licensing, conflicts, and editorial
authority have clear public processes and privacy-safe revision history.

### [Knowledge graph expansion](https://github.com/dougborg/ends-and-means/milestone/5)

The reviewed corpus broadens across traditions, institutions, places, people,
and depictions after the learner journey and evidence workflow have proven
durable.

## Delivery policy

Keep three to five implementation-ready issues in the Project and no more than
three implementation items in progress.
Reviews do not consume implementation WIP, but they must keep moving and cannot
be bypassed to start unlimited new work.
Blocked items name their unblock condition.
Issues remain focused and implementation-ready; milestones describe outcomes
rather than mirroring issue checklists in this file.
